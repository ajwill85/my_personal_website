## Jevons Paradox in the Age of AI

Jevons paradox is an economic theory that states that technological progress that increases the efficiency with which a resource is used tends to increase (rather than decrease) the total consumption of that resource.

## AI and Energy Consumption

AI is a computationally intensive technology that requires significant energy to train and run. As AI becomes more powerful and widespread, its energy consumption is expected to increase significantly.
